# OpenFML — product context

**Register**: product (design serves the tool).

**Users**: finance professionals, not programmers. Two personas: the
*modeller* (controller/FP&A lead who owns the model, works in the Studio
all day: grid, formulas, files, simulation, team) and the *contributor*
(department editor who fills granted cells in the Portal during a budget
round, then leaves).

**Scene**: a controller keys and reviews budget numbers for hours on a
bright office monitor, alongside Excel and email. Light theme, high
density, zero glare, long-session calm.

**Tone**: precision instrument, not SaaS marketing. The ledger, not the
dashboard. Numbers are the interface; chrome recedes.

**Anti-references**: navy-and-gold fintech templates; hero-metric
dashboards; identical card grids; Anaplan's 2010s enterprise chrome.
References for spirit: Runway (calm, human), Pigment (finance-friendly
density), classic print ledgers (hairline rules, tabular figures).

**Strategic principles**:
1. One accent, spent on "where am I / what can I act on" only.
2. Editability must be visible at a glance (warm paper cells) — the
   Alea/TM1 muscle memory users bring.
3. Every number tabular, right-aligned, row-uniform precision.
4. Hairline rules and typography carry hierarchy; boxes are rare.
